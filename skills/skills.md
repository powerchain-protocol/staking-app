# PowerChain AI Skills

Skills are capability policies, not unrestricted prompts. Every skill has a domain, tool allowlist and evidence requirement.

- `general.analysis` — explanation/synthesis; no fabricated facts.
- `solana.read` — read-only Solana RPC, Token-2022 and transaction analysis.
- `sui.read` — read-only Sui object/coin/bridge analysis.
- `powerchain.protocol` — staking, rewards, governance, bonds and network state.
- `renewables.analysis` — evidence-backed renewable infrastructure analysis.
- `charts.compose` — chart specifications from supplied or verified numeric data only.
- `data.ground` — RAG retrieval from trusted PowerChain sources.
- `support.help` — documentation/status-grounded support.

State-changing tools are outside the AI skill allowlist in v1.0.0.
